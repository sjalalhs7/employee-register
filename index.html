Interaction system

The prototype is deliberately separated from the character assets. A developer can replace the placeholder characters with GLB/GLTF models and connect the same interaction triggers.

Scroll choreography

Characters can travel between sections and trigger scene changes.

Reactive motion

Hover, click and touch actions can trigger chase, idle and reaction animations.

Responsive 3D

Desktop and mobile layouts are included with reduced-motion support.

Visual direction

Blue, green, purple, red and brown are the intentional visual palette — not character substitutes.

Primary depth, links and atmosphere.

Green

Energy and interactive accents.

Purple

Premium cinematic glow.

Red

Action and attention states.

Brown
